## Short description

## Full description

PackBack is a two-way checklist. Build a kit for a city break, a beach day, a trail, or a work bag. In Pack, tick only what you actually put in. Bring back checks those items, not the ones you left at home.

Mark something used up, left behind, or moved to another bag. Borrowed things stay labeled so you know what has to go back.

Looks, keep-screen-on, tap sound, and PackBack Pro (more than 8 trips) unlock with coins from the daily reward or 10 coin packs. Remove ads and PackBack Pro are one-time unlocks.

Remote config: https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N314.json — if disable=1, Play purchase sheets stay hidden. The shop still spends coins.
